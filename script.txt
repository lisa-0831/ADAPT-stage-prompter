# Scene 1 | Kansas Place in Brooklyn, New York
{DOROTHY Enters. TOTO, her service dog, is with her, played by an actor, wheelchair decorated like a basket.}
## SOMEWHERE OVER THE RAINBOW, WAY UP HIGH,
THERE'S A LAND THAT I HEARD OF ONCE IN A LULLABY.
SOMEWHERE OVER THE RAINBOW, SKIES ARE BLUE,
AND THE DREAMS THAT YOU DARE TO DREAM REALLY DO COME TRUE. ##
[DOROTHY]
Oh Toto, sometimes I fear I will never leave home.
I will just stay here on Kansas Place forever. Me. You. And My Chair.
{MS. GULCH enters furiously.}
[GULCH]
Where is that pest?!
[DOROTHY]
What do you want with Toto? He never did anything to you.
[GULCH]
He bit my leg. He belongs in the pound.
{AUNT EM & UNCLE HENRY enter from behind the house.}
[AUNT EM]
What are you doing here? Get the ffffunk out of here.
[GULCH]
I'll get you. And your little dog too.
{She drives away.}
[AUNT EM]
Don't be sad, Dorothy. Be happy. Don't worry about that old bat.
[DOROTHY]
I don't know why she is so mean. Maybe if we got to know her? Or brought her some flowers.

# Scene 2 | Munchkinland
[DOROTHY] 
Where are we? Toto, I don’t think we are in New York anymore.  At least my chair is fully charged! 
 
{Glinda appears in her flying coach/ bubble. Dorothy accidentally backs into her with her wheelchair.} 
 
[DOROTHY] 
Oh my god I did not mean to—are you hurt!? 
 
[GLINDA] 
You popped my bubble! I think my leg and maybe my arm are broken!
 
[DOROTHY] 
Oh no! I’m so sorry! We need to get you to a hospital! I don’t even know where I am? This is not New York is it? 
 
[GLINDA] 
I’ve never heard of New York. Calm, calm hon. Are you lost?  Are you a good witch or a bad witch?
